Frequently Asked Questions

Q
Does Hotel POS Extension support Odoo 20 Community Edition?
A
Yes, it is fully certified for Odoo 20 Community Edition, Odoo 20 Enterprise, and Odoo.sh. Custom third-party modules cannot be installed on Odoo Online (odoo.com SaaS) as it only supports standard Odoo apps and Studio.
 
Q
How do cashiers select a hotel room while placing an order in POS?
A
On the Point of Sale screen, cashiers click the dedicated Add Room button. This opens an interactive modal listing all active occupied rooms from hotel management. Selecting a room automatically assigns the registered guest and links the booking to the POS ticket.
 
Q
How does the "Pay at Checkout" payment option function?
A
Selecting the Pay at Checkout payment method routes the POS order balance directly to the guest's active room folio without requiring immediate card or cash payment. During checkout, front desk staff can compute the bill and invoice all charges together or separately.
